05 / GOOD TO KNOW

Quick questions.

Do I need to be an expert coder?+

No. This concept is designed around curiosity, teamwork, and learning by doing.

Can I come with an idea?+

Absolutely. A rough idea is a great starting point, and you can also join a team and explore together.

Is this a real event registration?+

No. CodeSprint 2026 is a fictional event created as a web-development recruitment task. The form is only a front-end demo.
